Senior Backend Engineer (Python) - Payments Platform

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to build and scale the services behind our payments platform, which
processes over 5 million transactions a day for merchants across India and Southeast Asia.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate high-throughput REST APIs and event-driven services in Python.
- Own services end to end: design reviews, implementation, testing, deployment and on-call.
- Model data and tune queries in PostgreSQL for correctness and performance.
- Work with product and compliance teams on reconciliation, refunds and settlement flows.
- Mentor junior engineers and raise the bar through code review.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with at least 3 years building backend services.
- Strong proficiency in Python and a modern web framework (FastAPI, Django or Flask).
- Solid experience with PostgreSQL or another relational database, including schema design and query optimisation.
- Experience with message queues or streaming systems such as Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Hands-on experience deploying containerised services with Docker on a cloud provider (AWS preferred).
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to have
- Experience in payments, fintech or banking systems.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes.
- Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).

What we offer
Competitive salary, ESOPs, flexible hybrid work from Bengaluru, and a learning budget.
